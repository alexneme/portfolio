## Day 1: Starting Off Right

**The challenge:** spend 90 days learning, in practice, what DevOps is and which tools help teams work better together, ship smaller releases more often, and automate what can (and should) be automated.

> DevOps isn’t a product, a magic piece of software, or a programming language. It’s a **way of working** that brings Development, Operations, and QA closer so we deliver value faster and more efficiently.

---

### The 90‑day plan (quick version)

- **12 weeks + 6 extra days.**  
- **First 6 days:** DevOps foundations (concepts, culture, and processes).  
- Then we dive into specific topics (tools and practices).  
- The goal is to **learn in public**: share what you learn and the materials that helped you.

Pro tip: sketch a **mind map** of what you want to learn and where you want to go. Start simple and refine as you go.

> [DevOps Roadmap — Mind‑map example](https://roadmap.sh/devops)

---

### What is DevOps? (no fluff)

- **It’s not a tool.** You don’t “buy DevOps.”  
- **It’s a set of practices and a culture** to shrink the time from idea to production.  
- **It helps everyone in the software cycle:** Dev, Ops, QA… and folks from infra as well.  
- **Real‑world goal:** instead of waiting months for a “big drop,” ship **smaller, continuous, reliable** releases.  
- **Break the silos:** less “each team on its own,” more **working together**.  
- **Automation is key:** the less repetitive manual work, the better.

Think of it like tuning the band **before** the show and agreeing on cues. The music (software) sounds better, the audience (users) is happier, and the show runs smoothly.

---

### DevOps & Agile: the dynamic duo

DevOps and Agile go hand in hand to enable **continuous delivery**. Agile helps organize work in short cycles; DevOps makes **build, test, and deploy** happen in an integrated, frequent way.

---

### Day 1 mission

1. **Define your personal goal** for the 90 days (e.g., “understand CI/CD and basic observability”).  
2. **Block ~1 hour a day** for the challenge. Videos, articles, docs—**consistency** is what matters.  
3. **Make your mind map** (paper, app, whatever you like) with the topics you want to cover.  
4. **Pick 1 intro resource** about “What is DevOps?” and write down 3 takeaways.  
5. **Share it:** post a short summary of what you learned (blog, LinkedIn, comments). Learning in public helps you and the community.

---

### To wrap up

DevOps is about **people, collaboration, and automation** to deliver value more often—with less drama. No mysticism: small steps, steady pace, clear purpose.

Let’s go? More tomorrow.
