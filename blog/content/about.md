I’m Alex. I fell in love with tech around age eight, tinkering with a GeForce 2 GTS, a Pentium II at 233 MHz, and a mighty 32 MB of RAM. I started on hardware, but in 2022 I found my home in **infrastructure**.

Since then I’ve focused on automation, containerization, observability, and networking—building systems that are secure, efficient, and scalable. Today I work as a cloud engineer with hands-on experience across **AWS**, **Oracle Cloud**, **Docker**, **Kubernetes**, **GitLab CI/CD**, **Cloudflare**, and databases like **PostgreSQL**, **MySQL**, and **MongoDB**. My goal is to remove friction and deliver predictability, lower costs, and speed for teams.
