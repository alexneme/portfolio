My name is Alex, and my passion for technology started around the age of 8, with a “super” machine: an Nvidia GeForce 2 GTS, a Pentium II 233 MHz, and 32 MB of RAM. Back then, my interest was fully focused on hardware, but in 2022 I truly discovered the world of software and, above all, infrastructure.

Since then, I’ve immersed myself in automation, containerization, observability, and networking—building environments that are more secure, efficient, and scalable. I have hands-on experience in AWS, Oracle Cloud, Docker, Kubernetes, GitLab CI/CD, Cloudflare, and databases such as PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MongoDB. My goal is to remove complexity from the way, delivering predictability, optimized cost, and speed for teams.

Nowadays, I work as a data engineer, designing and maintaining data pipelines (ETL/ELT) that integrate multiple sources and support applications at scale. I work with platforms like Snowflake, BigQuery, and Databricks, along with streaming and messaging tools such as Kafka and RabbitMQ. On a daily basis, I use Python and SQL to turn requirements into reliable solutions, always ensuring data quality, governance, and security.

At the end of the day, whether in cloud or data, what drives me is the same: using technology to simplify, accelerate, and generate real value for people and businesses.
